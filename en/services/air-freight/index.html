Air freight

Express and consolidated air cargo from DXB and DWC, with customs at both ends.

Get a quote

Air freight is the fastest way to move goods from the UAE when time matters more than cost: samples, spare parts, electronics, documents and high-value cargo.

How it works

We collect the cargo, check and pack it, prepare the export declaration and book space on the next suitable flight. On arrival our partners clear customs and deliver to your address.

When to choose air

  • Urgent or time-sensitive shipments
  • Small and medium volumes where sea consolidation would be too slow
  • High-value goods that need minimal handling

What’s included

  • Pickup anywhere in the UAE
  • Export declaration in Dubai
  • Consolidated and express options
  • Clearance and delivery on arrival
